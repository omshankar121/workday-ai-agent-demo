# Fictional Corp — HR Policy Handbook (excerpt)

This is mock content for a demo project. It does not represent any real company's policy.

## PTO Policy
Full-time employees accrue 1.75 paid time off (PTO) days per month, up to a maximum of 25 days per year. PTO must be requested at least 5 business days in advance for requests longer than 3 days. Unused PTO up to 5 days may be carried over into the next fiscal year.

## Expense Policy
All business expenses must be submitted within 30 days of the purchase date. Expenses over $75 require an itemized receipt. Client entertainment expenses require the names and companies of all attendees. Expense reports missing a receipt will be rejected and must be resubmitted with documentation.

## Remote Work Policy
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval. Fully remote arrangements require VP-level sign-off and are reviewed annually.

## Parental Leave Policy
Eligible employees receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave for birth, adoption, or foster placement, usable within 12 months of the qualifying event.

## Performance Review Cycle
Formal performance reviews occur twice a year, in March and September. Managers submit written feedback; employees complete a self-assessment beforehand.
